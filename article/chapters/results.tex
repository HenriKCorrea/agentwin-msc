\chapter{Results and Discussion}
\label{chap:results}

This chapter presents the results obtained from executing the evaluation defined in
    Chapter~\ref{chap:evaluation} and discusses their implications.
Results are organized along the same three dimensions as the evaluation methodology:
    semantic mapping evaluation,
    functional evaluation, and
    performance evaluation.
The discussion then interprets the findings collectively,
    connects them back to the research question,
    and situates them within the related work surveyed in Chapter~\ref{chap:related-work}.
